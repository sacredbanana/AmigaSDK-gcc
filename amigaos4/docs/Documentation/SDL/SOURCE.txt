SDL2 / SDL3 for AmigaOS 4 (newlib)
==================================

Installed under ppc-amigaos/SDK/local/newlib/{include,lib} and SDK/local/common/include,
which ppc-amigaos-gcc searches by default. Only the SDK/local/newlib and SDK/local/common
include/lib trees of each archive are installed (no docs, man pages or tools); clib2/clib4
builds were left out because SDL itself is only built for newlib.

lib/libSDL2.so and lib/libSDL3.so are links added here (the upstream installer creates them)
so that -use-dynld -lSDL2 / -lSDL3 link against the shared objects.
